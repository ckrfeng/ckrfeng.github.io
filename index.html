Research

Sparse autoencoder feature absorption

August 2026 – present · Independent research

I built an exact two-feature population model of sparse autoencoders that separates statistical co-occurrence from geometric feature alignment. The model investigates how sparsity penalties and dictionary capacity influence feature separation and absorption.

I derived normalization and scaling results for SAE objectives, identified a scaling degeneracy in unconstrained sparse reconstruction, and analyzed reconstruction–sparsity tradeoffs. The work distinguishes restricted candidate solutions from unrestricted global optima.

Finite-sample causal recovery from imperfect agent decisions

October 2026 – present · Independent research

I proved matching query-complexity bounds for recovering causal structure from idealized noiseless, zero-regret agent decisions, using metric packing and minimax analysis.

For imperfect decisions and corrupted observations, I derived information-theoretic lower bounds using binary-channel capacity and finite-sample recovery estimates, and created reproducible simulations to test recovery procedures.

Stability and bifurcation in Wu Xing and directed cyclic networks

October 2025 – present · Student researcher, Texas Southern University

At Texas Southern University, I develop modified Lotka–Volterra ordinary differential-equation models to investigate the dynamical stability of Wu Xing (five-element) networks. My analysis uses Fourier transforms, Green’s functions, and eigenvalue evaluations to study equilibria and robustness bounds.

I also proved positivity and global boundedness and derived Jacobian eigenvalue spectra using discrete Fourier transforms and circulant linear algebra.

I use XPPAUT and AUTO for numerical bifurcation analysis and phase-plane exploration, studying critical transitions, limit cycles, and stability thresholds. Python scripts support data analysis, statistical distribution modeling, and trajectory visualization.

I established dominant-mode selection and local bifurcation criteria, then investigated stability and perturbation dynamics through 90 × 90 parameter-grid sweeps, numerical ODE simulations, and 1,200-draw paired experiments.

Polynomial ergodic averages, Fourier analysis, and pointwise convergence

June 2026 – present · Independent research

I’m investigating almost-everywhere convergence of bilinear ergodic averages along the polynomial pair n² and n² + n. I established convergence for rational-spectrum and periodic components and am analyzing the interaction of quadratic and linear Fourier frequency scales.

I derived prime-modulus maximal estimates with p−1/4-type decay and power-of-two bounds of order (log q)1/2q−1/4 using Gauss sums and 2-adic stationary phase.

I also constructed counterexamples identifying limitations of a proposed inverse theorem and multiscale approaches. A fellow Ross participant audited and checked the work.

Projects

August – October 2026 · Evidence-based AI-agent learning

Model Learner

A local system that learns from AI coding-agent executions using independently verified task outcomes. It keeps versioned data records and reproducible execution histories, with automated test verification.

I developed hierarchical Bayesian models to predict agent success across tasks and configurations, testing uncertainty, calibration, and distribution shift with chronological experiments that avoid using future outcomes.

The system also uses task-similarity retrieval and cost-aware agent recommendations. I integrated it with the Suffice coding-agent harness and validated the combined workflow with 483 automated tests.

June – October 2026 · Finite-field & elliptic-curve hardware compilation

ECC Compiler

A compiler that translates finite-field operations and elliptic-curve point and scalar arithmetic into serial hardware state machines. It generates SystemVerilog and Calyx implementations over the small fields F17 and F19, allowing exhaustive verification.

I checked 4,477 hardware transactions and 120 abort/recovery tests against independent arithmetic references, and formally verified bounded completion for a Calyx multiplier.

Register allocation and arithmetic scheduling improvements reduced declared storage by 38.6% for point addition and 36.3% for scalar multiplication, while also reducing execution latency.

September – October 2026 · Causal reliability of SAE explanations

Shortcut Lens

A controlled MNIST image-classification benchmark investigating whether sparse autoencoders correctly explain the features a neural network actually relies on. The benchmark uses matched counterfactual images and independently controlled correlations.

I trained classifiers and sparse autoencoders, then compared causal feature interventions against supervised directions, PCA, and random baselines to measure causal influence, specificity, and preservation of task performance.

August – September 2026 · Collaborative project

Local Graph Assistant

An LLM-based application for navigating structured data. I co-trained Qwen3 0.6B and 1.7B LoRA models; the 1.7B model achieved 93.3% exact accuracy and 98% same-record accuracy on internal benchmarks.

I collaborated on a 16-crate Rust application with encrypted local storage, hybrid retrieval, and permission-controlled access to structured data.
