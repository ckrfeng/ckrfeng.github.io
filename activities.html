2023 – present · Founder & co-president

STEMJUMP

I founded STEMJUMP, a student-led nonprofit delivering affordable STEM programs to young students. I was elected co-president and have led 50+ members in teaching and organizing programs.

We’ve raised and donated more than $63,000 for local STEM education and conducted 125+ programs for 1,500+ students, including weeklong hands-on science and robotics camps.

Our in-person programs expanded to Fulshear, Houston, and Katy. Virtual classes have reached students across the country, including California, New York, Oregon, and Texas. I teach mathematics, physics, and chemistry to younger students.

I also published Science Experiments on Amazon, with 58 hands-on experiments. The book is used as a textbook for STEMJUMP camps and received a five-star rating from Readers’ Favorite.

Seven Lakes High School

Science Olympiad

Our Seven Lakes team won the 2025 National Championship and the 2026 Texas State Championship. I competed in Materials Science, Optics, Wind Power, and Forensics.
